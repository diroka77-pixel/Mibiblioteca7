# Bookhollow privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Bookhollow is an app for organizing and reading ebooks. It has no ads or analytics of its own and does not require an account. Google ML Kit features (ISBN scanner, translation and language detection) may send Google technical usage and performance data under Google's terms; never the content of your books or notes.

## Data it stores

The catalog, covers, reading progress, notes, highlights and preferences are stored on the device. If the user chooses a Google Drive folder or another provider supported by Android's document picker, the app only accesses the files the user authorizes, and stores a backup file of the catalog there.

Bookhollow turns off Android's automatic data backups. Backups are exported and restored only when the user decides.

## Internet use

The connection is used to sync with the chosen file provider and for features that look up public information when the user uses them: Open Library (book details, covers and new releases), Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wiktionary (book details, biographies, definitions) and Project Gutenberg (free classics). These lookups send only the terms needed, such as the title or author. For new releases, the names of major publishers and the language are sent; the device's country is only used on the phone to choose them and is not sent. Bookhollow has no server of its own that receives the user's library.

## Permissions

- Camera: the app does not request this permission. The ISBN scanner is provided by Google Play services, which shows the camera image without giving the app access to it.
- Microphone: only for voice search.
- Notifications and media service: to show the read-aloud controls.
- Internet: for lookups and syncs started by the user.

Optional permissions can be denied in Android; the rest of the app keeps working.

## External services and links

Buttons that open other websites (Goodreads, Casa del Libro, YouTube, the RAE…) only open them when the user taps them, and those websites apply their own policies. The Davefx voice is optional: Bookhollow opens the project's official page in the browser and does not request permission to install apps.

## User control

The user can export a backup, change or remove folder access from Android and delete Bookhollow's data from the system settings. The app never deletes books on its own.

## Purchases

Bookhollow Premium is bought with a one-time payment through Google Play. Google handles the payment: the app never receives or stores card or payment details; it only asks Google Play whether the purchase exists and stores on the phone that Premium is active.

## Children

Bookhollow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. For any privacy question or to request the removal of content, email bookhollow.app@gmail.com.
